## Why Care?

The Lossless tree has a splash page for nearly every repo worth knowing, and
nowhere listed them. Combing the tree found fourteen, every one live.

## What's New?

- **`/splashes/`**, a page of cards like `/sites/`: Lossless AI-Labs,
  Context Vigilance, Lossless Agent Skills, Lossless Flavored Markdown,
  Astro Knots, Content Farm, the context-v corpus, MemoPop AI, DidiDecks AI,
  Augment It, didi.sh, flave, event-hog and hope.ai. Each card links to the
  live page and, when the repo is public, the repo.
- **A "Splashes" link** in the nav, and links between `/sites/` and
  `/splashes/`.
- **One grid for both pages** (`HighlightGrid.astro`), reading two
  collections with the same card shape, so the two pages can't drift apart.
