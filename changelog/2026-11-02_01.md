## Why Care?

These two docs were sitting unpublished in the content repo, away from the code they describe. They now sit in Astro Knots' `context-v/`, where people working on these sites will look for them.

## What's New?

- **Blueprint:** [[blueprints/CSS-At-Rules-for-Theming.md|CSS At-Rules for Theming with Tailwind]]
- **Spec:** [[specs/Implement-Dynamic-Sort-and-Filter-with-Svelte.md|Implement Dynamic Sort & Filter with Svelte]]

## Under the Hood

Both docs meet the context-vigilance kit's OKF rules and pass its frontmatter check. Each now declares a `type`, has minted IDs and a description, and is listed in its folder's `index.md`. `To-Implement` isn't in the kit's status vocabulary, so the spec became `Draft` and the blueprint became `Reference`. The spec's filename dropped its ampersand to stay Train-Case. The body text didn't change.
