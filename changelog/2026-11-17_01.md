# Share Cards, a Table of Contents and Search Become Standard Equipment

## Why Care?

Most links to our sites get opened on a phone, in a chat thread. Until today a
link to almost any page on an Astro Knots site unfurled as the same generic
card, and a link to one section of a long article showed the whole article's
card, because chat apps ignore `#heading`. Long pages had no map, and search on
several sites looked broken even when it worked.

Two client sites fixed all of that today, cathode-site and edviro-blog, and the
fixes are now written down as the standard every site ships with.

## What's New?

- **Standard Equipment.** `README.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` now carry a
  table of what every site with long-form content ships from day one: a table of
  contents, share images, share buttons and search. Each row names its
  blueprint and the site to copy from, so the next site gets them without being
  asked.
- **A blueprint for share images,**
  `Share-Every-Page-and-Section-With-Its-Own-Image`: a tall 1080×1440 `og:image`
  and a wide `twitter:image` for every page and every section, built in the site
  at build time. It covers card design for a phone, share buttons people find,
  the "Link copied" toast, and the rules that bit us.
- **Proof in a real thread.** A Cathode write-up shared in iMessage came up as a
  clean tall card with its own photo and title:

  ![Cathode's tall share card for "Energy management is a data discipline, says Uptime": a globe photo across the top, a green kicker naming the 7x24 Exchange Fall 2026 Conference, the title in large white type, and a one-line summary](https://cathode.co/og/tall/log/energy-management-is-a-data-discipline-7x24-fall-2026.jpg)

- **Two reference sites.** cathode-site (dark brand, LFM) and edviro-blog (light
  brand, Astro MDX) both ship it, so a new site can start from whichever is
  closer.
- **The table of contents** blueprint gains cathode-site as its second
  implementation: a left rail lined up with the logo on wide screens, a menu on
  laptops, a bar naming the current section on phones.
- **Search** is the spill-out palette on Pagefind's JavaScript API, not the
  stock Pagefind UI.

## Under the Hood

- The first cards looked fine full size and wrong in iMessage: photos cropped
  to a streak, small type, an empty top half. That's written up as the issue
  `Share-Cards-Hid-Their-Photos-and-Shrank-Their-Type`, and its rules are folded
  into the blueprint.
- The older OG docs now agree with the new default.
  `Maintain-an-Elegant-Open-Graph-System` points its single 1200×630 default at
  per-page tall cards. The request-time Playwright plan in
  `Dynamic-OpenGraph-Images` is marked superseded. The iPhone tall/wide issue
  records that one tall `og:image` gives a clean card in iMessage.
- In the skills repo, `generate-consistent-og-images` now makes build-time
  per-page cards its default technique, and keeps Ideogram and hero screenshots
  for the site card.
