## Why Care?

The knot is wide, so in a square browser tab or on a phone's home screen it
looked small and off-balance. Held in a ring, it fills the square and still
reads at 16 pixels, and it changes color with the site's mode like the rest
of the page.

## What's New?

- **A ring mark.** The knot inside a ring, as `KnotMarkRing.astro` (live,
  reading the theme tokens) and as icon files, one per mode.
- **The ring in the header and the hero**, on every page, with the same slow
  "pulling thread" motion the knot had. The footer keeps the original knot.
- **A tidier "Build a site" section:** numbered steps on one line, the prompt
  in a card with its copy button and notes, and what your agent follows laid
  out underneath. The "Redesign a site" section now lines up with the rest of
  the page.
- **A favicon that follows the mode you pick.** It starts from your
  system's light or dark setting, then switches to light, dark or vibrant to
  match the site's toggle.
- **App icons:** an Apple touch icon, 192 and 512 pixel icons, and a web
  manifest.
- **A brand kit page** at `/brand-kit/` (kept out of search and the nav): the
  live knot, the ring in the current mode and as files, and all nine square
  explorations at app-icon and favicon sizes, in light and dark browser tabs.
- **One generator** (`scripts/build-knot-icons.mjs`) writes every mode's
  icon from one shape and one palette table, so they can't drift apart.

The original `favicon.svg`, `KnotMark.astro` and every exploration file are
kept as they were.
