# SEO Skills Worth Mining

The pack lives at `context-v/skill-packs/seo-skills/` (our fork of `Illyism/seo-skills`).
It is not linked into any harness. We read these skills to write our own, not to run them.

## Relevant

| Skill | Folder | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `seo-roast` | `seo/` | Blunt SEO teardown of a site and its code, ordered by revenue impact; fixes as a PR plus a roadmap. |
| `seo-title-optimization` | `seo/` | Audits and rewrites title tags and frontmatter titles; checks H1 alignment and truncation. |
| `internal-linking` | `seo/` | Links high-traffic posts to the pages that matter (`/pricing`, `/use-cases/*`, `/vs/*`). |
| `blog-refresher` | `seo/` | Six-phase audit for updating existing posts so they outrank competitors. |
| `directory-submissions` | `seo/` | Submits a product to directories, launch sites, MCP registries, and Awesome lists. |
| `unslop-seo` | `content/` | Cleans up AI-sounding drafts and restructures pages so AI answer engines cite them. Pairs with `humanizer`. |
| `intro` | `content/` | Short blog intros that match what the reader came for. |
| `understand-customers` | `copywriting/` | Buyer-psychology brief (pains, objections, the customer's own words) written before any copy. |
| `blitz-copy` | `copywriting/` | Landing pages, headlines, and outreach in a Hook, Spike, Payoff structure. |
| `customer-journey` | `marketing/` | Maps how customers find and compare a product across Google, AI chatbots, and social. |
| `parasite-and-pr` | `marketing/` | Press release, Dev.to/Hashnode repost, and LinkedIn version, bundled for publishing elsewhere. |

## Relevant, but need a paid service

| Skill | Folder | Service |
|---|---|---|
| `dataforseo-serp` | `seo/` | DataForSEO: live SERPs, keyword volume, AI Overview citations. |
| `ahrefs-research` | `seo/` | Ahrefs: competitor gaps and link targets. |
| `reddit-serp` | `seo/` | Finds Reddit threads ranking on page 1 for buying-intent keywords. |
| `honest-link-outreach` | `seo/` | Vetted backlink prospects with plain-text email drafts. |

## Also

| Skill | Folder | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `vs-and-alternatives` | `seo/` | Builds `/vs/[competitor]` and `[competitor]-alternative` pages from real pricing and switching triggers. |
