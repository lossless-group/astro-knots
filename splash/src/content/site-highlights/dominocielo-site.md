The inverse brief: nothing to cut, and a client who asked for simple. The design problem is making
brevity read as confidence rather than as an unfinished page.

Proof that creative latitude and hard constraints coexist — `WORD_BUDGET` is checked against each
direction's built HTML, so going over means copy was invented. Card, Sheet and Aperture take their
extra surface in typography, space and structure instead.
