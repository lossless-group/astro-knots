A materials-state-control company, one confidential deck, and three complete sites — Phase Diagram,
Lattice, and Before the Beam. The spine was written to `content/beats/` first, so three directions
could be built concurrently without any of them quietly rewriting the pitch.

Best demonstration in the workspace of **illustration as code**: six flare components drawn in SVG
and CSS against theme tokens, so they re-colour with the brand and never ship a stale raster.
