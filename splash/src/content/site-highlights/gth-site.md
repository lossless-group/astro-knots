The most demanding of the variant spikes, because the site has real work to do. Journal, Atlas and
Practice share the same collections and the same Svelte island — `BundleLadder` serves three cart
layouts from one component rather than three forks.

Source of two patterns that graduated into the shared context layer: the superset-tier cart that
shows a delta instead of a new cart, and the reminder to orient a reader before introducing a term
of art. Also the reference for the **Plate** imagery pattern — one image family off a locked seed,
dissolved by blend mode and tinted toward `--color-primary`, covering all nine theme/mode pairs.
