## Why it's here

Lightning Demo 2, after Cortado's firm-impact angle. Toby's demo brings a complementary perspective:

- **Different tools** — OpenClaw + Obsidian instead of Claude Teams. Audience sees that the agentic-VC toolkit isn't a monoculture.
- **Different problem** — pipeline vigilance and deal triage (the "stay current on what you've already seen" problem), not firm operations.
- **Different vantage** — a founder showing the workflow he uses to *be* a builder, which happens to also describe what an investor's pipeline should look like. The inversion is the lesson.

## What to surface

- **Toby's name + Ideem + Founder/CEO role**, distinct from the Cortado team's "Kauffman Fellow" chip. The audience should register *this is a founder demoing to VCs*.
- **The two-tool pair** — OpenClaw + Obsidian — with a one-line role for each (engine + memory). Logos if available.
- **The "vigilant pipeline management" phrase** — that's the load-bearing concept. Borrowed from the user's own framing.
- **The workflow-summary one-paragraph** — concrete enough that the audience can picture it before the demo starts. Not a list, a flow.
- **The "why a founder is demoing to VCs" framing** — explains the inversion. Audience should understand this isn't a tools talk; it's a "watch how a builder thinks about staying current" talk.
- **Time signature**: ~10 minutes, often runs long. Same caveat as 06a.
