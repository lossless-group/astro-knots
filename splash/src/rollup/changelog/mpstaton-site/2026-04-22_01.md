## Citations / Footnotes

- Added `footnoteReference` handling to `AstroMarkdown.astro` — reads `node.data.citationIndex` for sequential display number, falls back to raw identifier.
- Added `footnoteDefinition` suppression (rendered via Sources component instead of inline).
- New `Sources.astro` component renders `tree.data.citations.ordered` as a numbered list with linked titles, source domains, and published dates.
- Wired citations into `[...slug].astro` page template — extracts `tree.data.citations.ordered` from parsed MDAST and passes to `<Sources>`.

## Content

- Updated `why-markdown-needs-to-grow-up.md` with hex-code footnote references (`[^a1b2c3]` style) and structured definitions with title, URL, and published date.

## Dependencies

- Linked `@lossless-group/lfm` to workspace version (`workspace:^`) to pick up the new `remark-citations` plugin during development.
